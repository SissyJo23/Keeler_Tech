# 🔎 KeelerTech

> **Enterprise-grade verification infrastructure designed to cross-reference legal briefs, audit citations, and eliminate generative AI hallucinations before court submission.**

---

## 🛑 The Problem
Generative AI models are powerful but fundamentally ungrounded. Out-of-the-box language models frequently hallucinate completely fabricated case laws, statutes, and legal citations. Submitting these fabrications to a court results in severe professional liability, reputational ruin, and massive financial sanctions for law firms and Fortune 500 legal departments.

## 🛠️ The Solution
**KeelerTech** acts as a secure, real-world credibility assessment and verification layer. By decoupling the generative model from raw execution and introducing rigid data infrastructure pipelines, KeelerTech ensures that every argument, precedent, and citation is rigorously validated against factual database baselines before final documentation is processed.

### 🌟 Core Architecture Capabilities
* **Automated Citation Extraction:** Scans draft text files and maps out every embedded legal citation.
* **Factual Baseline Verification:** Cross-references extractions with validated federal and state registries to check for authenticity.
* **Deception Interception Feed:** Instantly highlights and flags hallucinated data points inside a split-screen workspace workspace before filing.
* **Compliance Audit Ledger:** Logs organizational data veracity scores for internal corporate governance.

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack & Setup
* **Frontend:** Clean, performant HTML5, CSS3 Variables, and vanilla JavaScript.
* **Database & Integrity Layer:** Designed for seamless integration with secure, relational RAG pipelines (Supabase/PostgreSQL).
